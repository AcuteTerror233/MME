## 📖 About

Might Makes Everything is a Fabric mod inspired by the legendary mod **MITE** (Minecraft Is Too Easy), rebuilt from the ground up with an even deeper survival experience.

You start with only **3 hearts**. You can barely break a block with your bare hands. Every tool must be crafted, every metal must be earned, and every step into the dark is a gamble.

> ⚠️ **Status**: The mod is still a work in progress and not yet fully complete. Only version **1.21.5** is fully playable from start to finish; builds for other Minecraft versions have only been adapted for compatibility, and their features are **not guaranteed** to work correctly.

---

## ❤️ Survival Overhaul

- Start with only **3 health points** and a hunger bar
- New **nutrition system**: Protein / Sugar / Fiber. Different foods provide different amounts — neglect your diet and suffer **malnutrition** or **diabetes** (insulin resistance)
- You are extremely fragile — without armor, *any* monster can easily kill you
- **Crafting takes time** — high-tier weapons, armor, and metal anvils take exceptionally long
- Health regenerates very slowly (**64 seconds per heart**) and even slower when malnourished; sleeping in a bed regenerates **8× faster** — you can also sleep during the day to recover
- Leveling requires far more XP, but the rewards scale: every **5 levels** grants **+1 max HP & hunger**, up to level 35 (vanilla standard)

## ⛏️ Mining & Gathering Restrictions

- Attack range drastically reduced (**1.5 blocks** unarmed) — weapons extend it
- Block interaction range: **3 blocks**
- Breaking blocks is *extremely* slow — most blocks cannot be destroyed by hand
- **Logs**: only short axes, axes, or battle axes
- **Stone & ores**: pickaxes or warhammers
- **Metal blocks**: only a pickaxe/warhammer of a *higher-tier* metal
- Most player-crafted portable/functional blocks can be picked up by hand

![Can't break by hand](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1f7bcc876b7ccb219152f31c3ba6e1d4ce0eb1f8.png)

## 🏹 Early Game Progression

- You start as a mere hunter-gatherer: hunt animals, gather plants, and craft food to survive
- Craft **flint tools** by hand to obtain wood
- Your first metal pickaxe comes from collecting **metal particles in gravel**
- Food drains faster (mining, fighting, etc.) — stock up enough to survive the night
- The metal workbench slows the pace — advancing through each metal tier shapes the whole game rhythm

## ⚒️ New Metals & Ores

- **4 new ores & 5 new materials**: Silver, Mithril, Ancient Metal, Adamantium (+ Rusted Iron)
- Metal tiers (low → high):
  **Gold → Copper/Silver (→ Rusted Iron) → Iron → Ancient Metal → Mithril → Adamantium → Netherite**
- Tiers impose extra restrictions on crafting, using, and repairing tools
- **Rusted Iron** is only obtainable from monsters; **Ancient Metal** only from Trial Chambers
- No artificial mineshafts early on — you must collect exposed ore in caves or on rocky shores
- Monsters deeper underground are more diverse and dangerous — deeper means richer, larger ore veins

![Ore veins](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/96a5affe3a269d8ed194052c43325bbf308542da.png)

## 🌌 The Underground Dimension

- Build a portal in the **bedrock layer** of the Overworld (bedrock must occupy the **bottom-left or bottom-right** corner) to enter the **Underground**
- The Underground has **two layers** separated by bedrock:
  - Mithril & diamonds spawn more frequently in the **second layer**
  - **Adamantium is exclusive to the second layer**
- Underground monsters are far more dangerous than Overworld ones
- The terrain is extremely complex — it's easy to get lost
- Find the **Mantle** at the lowest level of the Underground and build a portal with it (bottom-left/bottom-right corner) to enter the **Nether**
- The Nether is now far more dangerous, and entering it **requires passing through the Underground** first
- Trial Chambers & Ancient Cities now spawn **only in the Underground**; obtain **Rune Teleport Stones** from Trial Cages in the Hall of Trials
- **Rune Portals**:
  - Mithril Portal: teleports ~**8,000+ blocks** away
  - Adamantium Portal: teleports ~**20,000+ blocks** away

## 🔥 Furnaces & Fuel Heat Levels

- 5 new furnace types:
  | Furnace | Heat Level |
  |---|---|
  | Clay | 1 |
  | Sandstone | 1 |
  | Hardened Clay | 1 |
  | Obsidian | 3 |
  | Netherrack | 4 |
- Furnaces **extinguish** when blocked by opaque blocks
- Many new items can be used as fuel
- All wooden items, charcoal, and torches can cook meat in any furnace
- 4 fuel heat tiers — vanilla changes: Blast Furnace = 4, Smoker = 1, Furnace = 2
- Campfires burn only **80 seconds**, but can be extended by adding fuel
- Smelting requirements:
  - Common metals (copper/silver/gold/iron) → cobblestone-tier furnace + coal
  - **Mithril** → obsidian-tier furnace + lava
  - **Adamantium** & Netherite → Netherrack furnace powered by **Blaze Rods**
- Netherite remains the top tier, upgraded from Adamantium with a template
- Skeletons now have **ranged and melee variants** — fast and deadly

## ⚔️ New Tools & Weapons

- New arsenal: **clubs, knives, daggers, warhammers, battle axes, pickaxes (war picks), and sickles**
- Wooden (except shovels), stone, and diamond tools had their functions removed; the wooden sword recipe now gives a low-damage **wooden club**
- **Obsidian** can now be used to craft tools
- Almost all tool durability can't withstand heavy use
- **Flint axe** — the easiest early-game tool, crafted by hand to harvest logs
- **Copper pickaxe** — the easiest tool for mining stone and ore
- **Repairing** requires a same-tier (or higher) **metal anvil** and consumes **nuggets** — no XP needed. Anvils have durability now, and you can inspect it right in your inventory

## 🌾 Farming Overhaul

- **Blueberry bushes** in forests can be harvested repeatedly — blueberries are edible and used in other foods
- Crops have stricter growth requirements and grow **extremely slowly**
- New **manure system**: animals produce manure that enriches farmland
- Wheat yield reduced — harvesting gives only **1 wheat**

## 🏚️ World Changes

- Villages only spawn as **zombie villages**, with longer intervals — **no villagers, no crops**
- Strongholds only spawn **10,000+ blocks** from spawn
- Furnaces, workbenches, anvils, and enchanting tables become **unusable** if blocked by opaque blocks in front of or above them
- The closer monsters are to the bottom of the world, the more often they drop equipment
